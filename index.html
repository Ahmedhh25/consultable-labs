About Consultable Labs

An Honest Diagnosis, Then a Plan You’ll Actually Use.

Most leaders already know the symptoms. Attendance is slipping. The strategic plan sits on a shelf. The succession conversation stalls every time it starts. We begin a step earlier, with why, because the fix for a structural problem looks nothing like the fix for a people problem.

From there we build something workable: clear roles and decision authority, priorities your board can act on, and enough follow-through that the work outlasts the engagement. Based in Raleigh, North Carolina, working with organizations across the United States.
